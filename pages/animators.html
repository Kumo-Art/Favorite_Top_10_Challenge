1. Norio Matsumoto

Norio Matsumoto is most well known for his animation work on Naruto's fight scenes, his most well known techniques are how he bends and stretches the antomy and cares less structural accuracy in favor of animation fluidity.

2. Vincent Chansard

Vincent Chansard is one of the top key animators that works on One Piece, most known for how much color he uses, as well as his use of impact frames.

3. Spencer Wan

Spencer Wan most known for his work on the Castlevania animated series, he uses a technique seen in the video with an all black animation style.

4. Weilin Zhang- twitter info: Weilin Zhang

Weilin Zhang another animator who works on Naruto, most known for his work on the fight scene here.

5. Saucelot - twitter info: Saucelot

Saucelot this is their twitter name, most known for their work on Jujutsu Kaisen, they use a really abstract paiterly animation style, with less detail and more fluidity

6. Ryoma Hattori

Ryoma Hattori most well known for their work on My Hero Academia, signified by their use of very sketchy lines in their animation

7. Akihiro Ota

Akihiro Ota anither big One Piece animator, popular by this scene, technique shown through the fluidity of this scene.

8. Yutaka Nakamura

Yutaka Nakamura most known for their work on My Hero Academia and One Punch Man, most well known technique, is when animating explosions, shown by the tiny cubes when animating explosions.

9. Arifumi Imai

Arifumi Imai most known for their work on Attack on Titan, shown in this iconic scene, note-worthy technique is their use of camera angle switching.
