My Top 10 Favorite Video Games

This is a List of my Top 10 favorite Video Games. It was hard to rank these games because I enjoy them so much

The List:

#1 The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth

The Reason why I have this game ranked first is because it is my favorite game of all time, and have many memories playing it from over the years.

#2 Plants vs. Zombies

The reason why this game is placed here is because it was one of the first video games I have played as a child and have many memories from it. I also enjoy many aspects of the game such as the music, character designs, artstyle, etc.

#3 Skylanders: Giants

The reason why this game is also placed here is because this was also one of the first video games I have played. Skylanders is one of my favorite game franchises and Giants is my favorite game in the franchise.

#4 Pokemon Ultra Moon

Pokemon is also one of my favorite game franchises and Ultra Moon happens to be my favorite pokemon game. Ultra Moon is my favorite pokemon game due to it having more challenging battles, most of my favorite pokemon are obtainable in the game,

#5 Xenoblade Chronicles

Xenoblade Chronicles is my 2nd favorite Nintendo franchise next to Pokemon, and I was having trouble either choosing between Xenoblade 1 and 2, but I decided to choose Xenoblade 1 because it was the first Xenoblade game I played. I placed it at #5 because I would say it is one of my favorite JRPGs of all time and I think #5 is a high place on the list.

#6 Kingdom Hearts

Kingdom Hearts is one of my favorite action RPGs and I loved many things in the game such as the fun combat and attack combos I can do, as well as the interesting story the game has. I would say the reason it's placed here is because sometimes the exploration would be difficult in 2-3 of the explorable worlds in the game.

#7 Persona 4 Golden

I chose for Persona 4 Golden to be on the list because this is my favorite Persona game in the franchise. The reason why it is placed at #7 is because despite me replaying it a few times, I have more memories with many of the other games on this list.

#8 Deltarune

originally I was going to place Undertale here but I decided to put Deltarune here instead despite me loving both games Equally. in the end, I chose for Deltarune to be here because I enjoyed the gameplay more. I placed Deltarune at #8 because although I did enjoy the game as I played it multiple times, the other games on the list felt more in depth when it comes to RPGs.

#9 Garry's Mod

I placed Garry's Mod on the list because it is my favorite sandbox type games, and I enjoy playing the game, using different mods, and doing many different things in the game. Im pretty sure I have around 70gb worth of mods installed onto the game, which I find to be funny. I placed it at #9 because despite me having lots of stupid fun with the game, I found the other games on the list more enjoying.

#10 Risk of Rain 2

The Reason why Risk of Rain 2 is placed here is because I enjoyed the other 9 games more compared to this game. This doesnt mean I dislike the game or anything, but I had more fun with the other 9 games.
